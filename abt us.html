Our History

Bakers Bastion was founded in 2009 by 4 passionate bakers who came together because of their shared love for baking. At first, they were students baking in their free time. Their passion eventually became a successful cake shop known for unique and delicious cakes.

Timeline of our journey.
2009: Bakers Bastion was founded by 4 passionate baking students.
2012: The first physical bakery was opened in singapore.
2015: Bakers Bastion expanded to include a wider variety of cakes and patries.
2018: Bakers Bastion started offering online ordering and delivery services.
2026: Bakers Bastion serves thousands of happy customers and contiune to grow their reputation

Our Mission

We are committed to serving cakes with quality, tradition and taste, turning every bite into a memory.

Our vision is to become Singapore's favourite cake shop, creating unforgettable memories through our cakes.
